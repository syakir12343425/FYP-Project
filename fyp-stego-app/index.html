Mathematical Threat Vector Details

Bicubic Resizing: interpolates pixel intensities using a 16-point cubic convolution kernel: W(x) = (a+2)|x|³ - (a+3)|x|² + 1, for |x| ≤ 1 This acts as a high-cut low-pass filter, erasing spatial LSB bit arrays (99% information loss). A robust CNN encodes information inside low-frequency structural components to survive this kernel convolution.

3. Methodological Discussion

Under LSB steganography, data bits are overwritten sequentially into the 8th bit of pixel values. Resizing operations average these matrices out, obliterating the spatial bits. Conversely, the CNN Encoder-Decoder employs deep residual learning with sub-pixel interpolation simulator layers during training. The CNN embeds message signatures in structural patterns (e.g., textures, edges) rather than discrete pixels, allowing the decoder to successfully recover the message from downscaled and interpolated blocks.
